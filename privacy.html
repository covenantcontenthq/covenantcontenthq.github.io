Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Covenant Content Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

Information the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives OAuth access to the selected YouTube channel. It uses this access only to upload videos, set video titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy status and scheduled publish times, and read basic channel information (channel ID, title and handle) to confirm it is posting to the correct channel.

How information is stored

OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer and are used only by the owner's automation. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or no longer needed.
